# US17 — /flow como prompt: reescritura prompt-engineered

## Execution prompt (Phase 3 input)

**Task**: Rewrite `flow.md` from an over-constrained prose prompt into a slim state-machine router with ONE short executable checklist per phase boundary, ticked into state.json (verifiable compliance), cutting instruction volume and mandate count.
**Context (measured 2026-08-05, prompt-engineer audit)**: flow.md = 2,795 words; full chain (flow + 6 phase SKILL.mds + drillme + skill-advisor) = **23,138 words ≈ ~31k tokens of instructions** per full run, BEFORE the references each skill mandates reading and before the ~7k always-loaded base. Mandate density: **98 MUST/MANDATORY/SIEMPRE/NEVER across the chain**. Measured compliance of those mandates: skill-advisor mandated at every phase boundary (≈98 expected across 14 lifecycles) → **2 launches ever (~2%)**; drillme at 3 points (≈42 expected) → ≤8 (~19%); retro mandated always → retro approved 4/14 (29%). Rubric score of flow.md as prompt: ~56/100 (<70 → improve): Structure 8/20 (obligations scattered across 8 files, no executable sequence per boundary), Actionable 8/20 (aggregate mandate load exceeds what any model reliably executes — each extra MUST beyond saturation lowers TOTAL compliance), Clarity/Context fine individually. This validates the user's hypothesis verbatim: "demasiado verboso, consume demasiado, te lías y no haces todo lo que pido".
**Constraints**: (1) flow.md target ≤1,200 words: triage + state schema pointer + per-boundary checklist contract; procedural prose (resume logic detail, edge cases, anti-patterns) moves to `references/` loaded on demand — same MIGRAR-Y-CUT pattern as tech-plan. (2) Per-boundary checklist: MAX 5 items, each mechanically checkable, ticked into state.json (new optional field `boundary_checks`) — compliance becomes measurable, not aspirational. (3) Mandate diet: consolidate the 98 into ≤25 across the chain — every kept mandate must be either checklist-item (verifiable) or hook-enforced (mechanical); prose-only mandates get demoted to guidance or deleted. skill-advisor: collapse from "every boundary" to 2 checkpoints (entering Phase 2, entering Phase 3) where the 024 research showed auto-activation weakest — the 98-point mandate produced 2% compliance, evidence that more mandates ≠ more compliance. (4) Preserve: hard gates 1→2/2→3 human-only, close-feature guard, drillme at gate closes (gap-gated), US12's re-enganche additions (this US runs AFTER US12 to consolidate its lines — no double rewrite). (5) Phase SKILL.md slimming is OUT of scope here (goes with US14 census) — but flow.md must stop duplicating what phase skills already mandate (single owner per mandate, Cmd IX). (6) Behavioral change → golden-prompt evals + before/after word/mandate counts reported in the PR body.
**Deliverable**: `flow.md` rewritten (≤1,200 words) + `commands/references/flow-*.md` (o dir equivalente) + `flow-state.ts` extensión `boundary_checks` (+ test) + counts antes/después.
**Verify**: `wc -w flow.md` ≤1,200; `grep -c` mandatos ≤25 en cadena flow.md; `bun test ./.claude/hooks/` + evals green; smoke: /flow --minimal sobre tarea trivial recorre checklist y state.json registra `boundary_checks`.
**Ask first**: ratificar el recorte de skill-advisor a 2 checkpoints (el dato de 2% avala; alternativa: mantener mandato amplio y aceptar no-cumplimiento — desaconsejado).

## User story

- **As a**: Oriol
- **I want**: que /flow sea un router fino con checklists cortas y verificables por frontera, no 31k tokens de mandatos que ni Claude cumple
- **So that**: el pipeline consume menos, no "se lía", y el cumplimiento se puede medir en state.json en vez de suponerse

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1**: Given flow.md reescrito, when `wc -w`, then ≤1,200 palabras; y mandatos (MUST/SIEMPRE/NEVER/MANDATORY) en flow.md ≤10, en la cadena ≤25.
- **AC2**: Given cada frontera de fase, when se cruza, then existe checklist ≤5 items mecánicamente comprobables y `flow-state.ts` los registra en `boundary_checks` (test red→green del campo).
- **AC3**: Given un mandato conservado, when se revisa, then es checklist-item o hook-enforced — cero mandatos prosa-only nuevos (tabla de clasificación en el PR body).
- **AC4**: Given skill-advisor, when se consolida, then queda en 2 checkpoints con el dato de cumplimiento citado; drillme conserva sus gate-closes gap-gated.
- **AC5**: Evals + hooks tests green; counts antes/después reportados.
